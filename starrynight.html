Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

The Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's greatest masterpieces. It features a beautiful night sky filled with swirling stars over a peaceful village. The painting is famous for its vibrant colors and expressive style.
